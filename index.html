TARGET PROFILE · VERIFIED DATA

NTU Medicine — MBBS

Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine · Singapore

Programme

Undergraduate Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS). Current LKCMedicine admissions information is maintained by NTU.

Application structure

Academic qualifications + UCAT + Personal Statement + Referee Reports + MMI. Selection requirements can change by admission year.

Student planning principle

APLUS separates official requirements from planning factors such as academic trajectory, leadership, service and medicine-related exposure.

Data status

Current official-source snapshot. Every requirement should be re-checked against the target entry year before an application is submitted.

A-Level

  • Pass in H2 Chemistry
  • Pass in H2 Biology or Physics

IB Diploma

  • Pass in HL Chemistry
  • Pass in HL Biology or Physics

Polytechnic

A good GPA in a Health Science-related and relevant diploma is stated by NTU.

Application components

  • Personal Statement: not more than 300 words
  • Two referee reports
  • Health requirements if offered admission
  • Criminal Record Declaration

Source: NTU LKCMedicine Entry Requirements

UCAT

NTU states that only UCAT results taken in the 12-month period prior to admission are considered. For AY2026–27, NTU gave the example of a test date between July 2025 and September 2025.

MMI

Applicants may be shortlisted for the Multiple Mini Interviews around April each year. MMI performance is part of the admissions selection process.

Source: NTU Entry Requirements · NTU Admissions FAQs

LKCMedicine Scholarship

Singapore Citizens; outstanding academic achievements plus contributions/attributes such as community service, leadership or sporting excellence. Full subsidised tuition, S$6,500 annual living allowance, S$2,000 one-off computer allowance and other listed benefits. No separate bond apart from the 5-year MOH bond.

Needs-based scholarships

Current AY2026–27 LKCMedicine financial-aid material lists Toh Kian Chui, Irene Tan Liang Kheng, Sim Yung Chong Medical and Temasek Foundation–G Muthukumarasamy under needs-based scholarships.

Award-count rule

Not publicly disclosed ≠ zero. APLUS records annual award counts only when an official source discloses them, and separates annual new awards from historical/current-student recipient counts.

Application

Scholarship applications are submitted through the admissions process. NTU states that application and MMI performance are considered; there is no additional scholarship interview for LKCMedicine scholarships.

Sources: LKCMedicine Scholarship · AY2026–27 Financial Aid PDF

Planning cycle

  1. Build academic foundation
  2. Check target-year academic eligibility
  3. Plan UCAT within the valid window
  4. Prepare application, referees and personal statement
  5. Prepare for MMI
  6. Track offers and waitlist

APLUS recommendation

Do not wait until JC2 to begin. The machine should start with the student's target entry year and work backwards to create milestones for academics, UCAT, communication, leadership, service and exposure.

TARGET PROFILE · VERIFIED DATA

NUS Medicine — MBBS

Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine · Singapore

Places

NUS Medicine states that it offers 280 places annually.

Assessment

From 2026, the FSA is the admissions assessment; the SJT is discontinued.

A-Level pathway

From AY2026, NUS states that UAS considers 3 H2 content subjects plus GP, with H2 Chemistry and H2 Biology or Physics required for Medicine.

UCAT

For Singapore A-Level applicants, NUS states that a supplementary qualification such as UCAT is not required.

A-Level

  • Good H2 pass in Chemistry
  • H2 Biology or Physics
  • Pass in Project Work from AY2026

Other qualifications

Requirements differ by qualification. NUS provides separate prerequisites for Polytechnic, IB, NUS High and other qualifications.

Source: NUS Medicine Admission Requirements

Focused Skills Assessment

The FSA assesses aptitude for Medicine and whether candidates' values align with NUS Medicine. NUS says candidates are not tested on academic or medical knowledge during the FSA.

Selection

NUS states that shortlisted candidates are ranked based on FSA performance, with 280 places offered annually.

Source: NUS Medicine Admissions Assessment

Portfolio

NUS lists a personal statement, CCA list, official testimonial and referee reports as portfolio components.

Assessment routes

NUS currently describes a Standard Scheme and an Excellence Beyond Academics Scheme (EBAS). Exact eligibility and submission windows should be checked for the target year.

Source: NUS Medicine Portfolio

Typical annual sequence

  1. Submit NUS application
  2. Submit Medicine portfolio
  3. Shortlisting around late March
  4. FSA in April
  5. Offers around late April–early May
  6. Acceptance around end May

Planning rule

Use the target admission year as the anchor. Dates shown here are based on current NUS information and must be rechecked for the student's actual cycle.
